Full Sensor Suite

Everything VitalSync tracks for you.

Three medical-grade sensors, an ESP8266 processor and an AI pipeline — working together to give you a complete picture of your physiology in real time.

ECG Waveform

The AD8232 sensor captures the heart's electrical signals with clinical precision, enabling full beat-cycle analysis on every reading.

  • QRS complex detection and measurement
  • P-wave and T-wave identification
  • Real-time arrhythmia flagging via 1D-CNN
  • PR, QT, and RR interval calculation

Heart Rate Variability

HRV is the most sensitive indicator of recovery status. VitalSync computes RMSSD and SDNN from every consecutive heartbeat pair.

  • Beat-to-beat R-R interval tracking
  • RMSSD and SDNN computation
  • Parasympathetic balance index
  • Trend graph across training blocks

Fall Detection

The ADXL345 accelerometer continuously watches for sudden deceleration events that indicate a fall, triggering instant alerts.

  • SVM model trained on fall signatures
  • 2.5g threshold trigger with timestamp
  • Distinct from high-intensity motion
  • Caregiver alert within 500 ms

Activity Tracking

Tri-axis acceleration patterns are classified into intensity zones — from resting to high-intensity — giving you a complete session picture.

  • Dynamic step estimation from raw accel
  • Rest / Light / Moderate / Intense zones
  • Per-session active minute summary
  • Caloric expenditure estimation

Ambient Temperature

Environmental temperature directly affects athletic performance. VitalSync logs every reading and flags dangerous deviation from your comfort zone.

  • 0.5°C resolution DHT11 readings
  • Heat stress threshold alerts
  • Temperature-correlated HR overlay
  • Environmental fatigue index scoring

Humidity Monitoring

Humidity is the often-overlooked variable in athlete fatigue. VitalSync correlates high humidity with elevated heart rate to predict heat exhaustion risk.

  • DHT11 relative humidity tracking
  • Sweat-rate impact estimation
  • Combined heat-index calculator
  • Comfort zone boundary notifications
AI/ML Engine

From raw signal to clinical insight

VitalSync runs a multi-stage machine learning pipeline on every sensor reading. ECG data is preprocessed, windowed, and passed through a 1D Convolutional Neural Network trained on the PhysioNet MIT-BIH dataset for arrhythmia classification.

Accelerometer data feeds an SVM classifier for fall detection. XGBoost and LSTM models aggregate cross-sensor features to compute recovery scores and flag multi-variable risk patterns.
