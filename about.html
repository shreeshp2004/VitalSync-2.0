Our Story

Built from frustration. Driven by data.

A $15 device. Three sensors. One mission: make real-time cardiac monitoring accessible to every athlete, not just the ones with $400 monitors.

The Problem
1 in 5
athletes experience undetected cardiac irregularities.
Cardiac Monitoring Gap

Most wearables report heart rate as a smoothed average — not the raw electrical signal. That difference matters enormously when you're trying to detect arrhythmias, HRV dips, or prolonged QT intervals during a sprint block.

Consumer smartwatches cost $300–$500 and offer approximate readings. Clinical Holter monitors cost upwards of $1,500 and require a physician to interpret results days later. Neither serves the everyday athlete.

VitalSync was born from the question: what if you combined a $3 ECG module, a $2 accelerometer, a $1 temperature sensor, and an ESP8266 WiFi chip — and put a real machine-learning pipeline on top? The answer is a device that costs under $15 to build and delivers real-time, physiologically accurate data straight to a web dashboard.

Tech Stack

Three layers. One system.

IoT Layer

The ESP8266 reads sensors, renders data on the OLED display, and POSTs structured JSON payloads to the backend every 500 ms over Wi-Fi.

ESP8266 AD8232 ADXL345 DHT11 HTTP/MQTT

Backend

Node.js server with Socket.io pushes live data to connected dashboards in real time. TimescaleDB stores time-series sensor payloads efficiently.

Node.js Socket.io TimescaleDB REST API

AI / ML

1D-CNN (TensorFlow Lite) classifies ECG rhythms. SVM detects falls. XGBoost computes recovery scores. FastAPI serves inference endpoints.

TensorFlow FastAPI XGBoost 1D-CNN SVM
