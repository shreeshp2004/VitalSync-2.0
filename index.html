The Pipeline

From Sensor to Insight in Milliseconds

01

Sense

AD8232 captures ECG signals, ADXL345 reads 3-axis acceleration, DHT11 monitors ambient temperature and humidity — simultaneously, in real time.

02

Transmit

The ESP8266 WiFi module POSTs sensor data to the cloud every 500 ms over HTTP. Low latency, high reliability, instant availability on the dashboard.

03

Analyze

AI models detect arrhythmias, score HRV, flag fall events and compute recovery indices — all in under one second, surfaced directly to the dashboard.

Core Features

Built around what your body tells you

AD8232 Sensor

Medical-grade heart rhythm analysis

The AD8232 module captures your heart's electrical activity beat-by-beat. Our 1D-CNN model flags arrhythmias in real time with 99.2% accuracy.

  • Beat-to-beat interval tracking
  • Real-time arrhythmia detection
  • QRS complex analysis
  • HRV calculation from R-R intervals
ADXL345 Accelerometer

Know every move. Catch every fall.

The ADXL345 tracks acceleration across 3 axes. Sudden impact spikes above 2.5g trigger an instant fall alert with location timestamp.

  • 3-axis acceleration tracking
  • Fall detection via SVM model
  • Activity intensity scoring
  • Motion-gated data filtering
